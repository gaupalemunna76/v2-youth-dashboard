    Answers repeated word for word

    The same answer (4 or more words) written by 2 or more different people. Short common answers like "Teacher" are not counted. Many copies of a long answer can mean people copied from each other or from one source.

    How the answers were sorted

    • Who is counted: young people aged 12 to 25. Duplicate submissions (the same answers sent twice) are counted only once.
    • Which answers are judged: only questions the person was actually asked. A question someone never reached because they stopped earlier is counted under "Where people stop", not here.
    • Good answer: answers the question in line with what the question was looking for (from the Tool Design sheet). A short answer can still be good, e.g. "Doctor" to "What do you want to be?".
    • Too short / vague: "No", "Nothing", "Yes", "Don't know", or a few generic words such as "good life" or "successful".
    • Off-topic / unclear: there is an answer, but it does not answer this question, or the voice-to-text made it hard to read.
    • Looks AI-written: long polished text with typical chatbot phrases ("foster", "holistic", "it is essential…").
    • Left blank: the person reached the question but gave no answer, or wrote "NA" / "skip".
    • For Mitra the first reply and the replies to Mitra's follow-up questions are read together.
    • Who decided: first, simple rules sorted every answer by looking for the words a good answer usually contains. A check (below) showed these rules are reliable when they say "good answer", but they often missed real answers that used other words. So every answer the rules did not call good () was then read for its meaning by reviewers using the same definitions. Their judgement is the one shown in this dashboard.
